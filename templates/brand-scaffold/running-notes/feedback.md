# Feedback (this brand)

Brand-specific feedback — corrections, preferences, what worked or didn't for *this* brand.
Separate from the `static-ads` skill's own `feedback.md`, which is generic across every
brand the skill runs for. Split "per template" and "whole brand," same pattern as the skill's
feedback file.

## Per template

Templates from the master library (`brain-system/creative-strategy-context/swipe-library/`) that don't
work for this brand, one line each, so the static-ad skill skips them. Format:
`<category> <id> — why — date`, e.g. `testimonial 6 — how-to steps don't fit a skincare jar — 2026-10-01`.
