## What the numbers point at
Which part to change, read from the ad's own data against the account's average:
- **Low hook rate** (few past 3 seconds): new hooks; the body may be fine
- **Good hook rate, low hold rate** (few past 15 seconds): the bridge from hook to body, or the
  first seconds of the body
- **Holds well, doesn't convert:** the product comes in too early, the proof is weak, or the offer
- **Was a winner, now fatigued:** new hooks on the same body first, then the same message in another
  format
If the concept itself is failing (several ads, none work), this isn't an iteration: change the angle
in `idea-bank/index.md` first, then the offer, then the persona, format last.
