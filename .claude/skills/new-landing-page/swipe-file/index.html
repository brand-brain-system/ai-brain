Landing page swipes

What 8 of the biggest Parker accounts send their paid traffic to. That's 11 pages, captured on phone and desktop 2026-10-01, grouped into 5 templates, plus one Parker-built page Alex chose to keep (marked "Parker build"). Each card shows the phone version: scroll inside it to see the whole page. Click it to open the desktop version, full length. To make one for a brand, press Build this one on its card and paste the copied line to Claude with the brand's name.

Spend is summed from each brand's top 40 ad groups by 90-day Meta spend (2026-07-01 to 2026-09-28). It's Meta-reported, and no page comparison here is a controlled test. The full breakdowns, master outlines and gaps are in README.md and the brands/ folder.
