# lp-02 Founder-story long page

**Use when:** Problem-aware, older buyers who have tried everything; the product has a mechanism story and the founder is the proof. Clone it per symptom or audience.

**Buying phase it serves:** Trigger to Evaluation

**The brand needs:** founder_story, mechanism, offer, product_page_or_checkout_url. **Helps to have:** reviews, before_after, result_timeline, comparison_alternative, guarantee.

## Why it works

A long sales letter that sits in the problem first. The founder lived it, tried everything the reader has tried, found the mechanism, and turned it into an easy form. Proof, a timeline, who it is not for, and a buy box that prices every freebie. Primal Queen puts over half its top spend behind one of these and clones it for each symptom.

## Adaptation notes

No founder story on record: this template is the wrong pick, use lp-03 (first-person) or lp-01. No before/after rights: drop it, never simulate. Urgency banned: the offer bar states the offer without a clock.

## Watch-outs

Long pages suit 45+ buyers who read; keep sentences plain. Health brands keep every claim inside the approved wording.
