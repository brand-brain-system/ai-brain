# lp-05 Offer lander

**Use when:** The ad sells a specific trial, free gift or limited drop. The headline is the offer, word for word.

**Buying phase it serves:** Purchase

**The brand needs:** offer, price, product_page_or_checkout_url. **Helps to have:** rival_price, reviews, clinical_or_proof, gift_options.

## Why it works

A short, standalone page that opens on the ad's own value line and sells one offer: a $10 trial, a product plus a free gift, a limited variant. Harry's runs at least four test versions behind one URL; Laura Geller's version drops the site menu entirely.

## Section contract

| # | Section | Required | Its job | What to write |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | offer bar | yes | Review strip or offer line | text 5-12 words |
| S02 | hero | yes | The offer's value line as the headline (the ad's own line, when an ad is named), the offer as the button | headline 4-10 words (the offer line, or the named ad's line); subhead 8-20 words (pain relief); cta names the offer, 3-6 words; trust: shipping and money-back; image: product or kit |
| S03 | trust row | yes | Four short promises | 4 items, 2-5 words each |
| S04 | who for | optional | Who it's for: three specific frustrations | for: 3 items of 5-12 words; not_for optional |
| S05 | buy box | yes | Offer card: price, anchor, savings, what's in the kit or the gift choice | options 1-3; includes 3-6; gifts with values if real; cta 3-6 words |
| S06 | stat strip | optional | Headline proof stat | 1-3 allowlisted |
| S07 | comparison table | optional | Price comparison vs a named rival, dated | 3-5 rows; note carries the source and date |
| S08 | reviews | optional | Three short reviews on the main pain | 3 verbatim |
| S09 | faq | yes | Offer terms and product questions | 4-6 Q&A |
| S10 | cta block | yes | Final call to action with the price stack | heading 4-10 words; body 10-25 words; cta |

## Adaptation notes

Limited drop: add the 'almost gone' line only if stock is really limited. Free-gift offer: the buy box lists gift options with their values.

## Watch-outs

Test the page like an ad: Harry's rotates story, engineering and value versions behind one URL.
