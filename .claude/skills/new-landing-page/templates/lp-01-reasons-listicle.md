# lp-01 Reasons-why listicle

**Use when:** Many creators and angles feed one page, and the buyer needs several reasons, not one. Works for Exploration into early Evaluation.

**Buying phase it serves:** Exploration to Evaluation

**The brand needs:** offer, product_benefits, product_page_or_checkout_url. **Helps to have:** reviews, customer_count, rival_comparison, expert_or_author, guarantee.

## Why it works

A numbered article that feels like content. Each reason is one benefit with one piece of proof and its own button, so a reader can leave for the product at the moment they are convinced. The Perfect Jean sends most of its traffic to a standalone 10 reasons page; Rugiet runs the same template for two products; mindbodygreen makes the last reason "results build over months" to sell the bigger plan.

## Section contract

| # | Section | Required | Its job | What to write |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | offer bar | yes | State the discount and that it is already applied | text: 6-12 words |
| S02 | byline | optional | Make it read like an article from a real person | name, title (relatable, 2-6 words), updated (month year); only a real author or the brand's own editorial name |
| S03 | hero | yes | Headline that states the page's promise as N reasons (the named ad's promise, when there is one) | headline: "N reasons [who] [love/switched to] [product]" or "Why [expert type] recommend [product] for [problem]", 6-14 words, in the buyer's words (keep the ad's voice when an ad is named); subhead: one-line product truth, 10-20 words; bullets: 3 proof points of 3-8 words; cta: 2-5 words; image: product in use |
| S04 | comparison table | optional | Show the win against named or typical rivals at a glance | columns: criterion + us + 1-2 rivals; 4-6 rows of short cells; note: one-line TL;DR, 8-20 words; only true, sourced rival facts |
| S05 | numbered reasons | yes | One benefit, one proof, one button per reason | 5-10 items; title 4-10 words; body 25-60 words with one number or proof; cta 2-5 words, a different verb each time; order: core benefit or speed first, fit or range, reviews and repeat buying, guarantee, lasting results, price vs rivals, zero risk or social payoff last |
| S06 | stat strip | optional | One big proof number and a short quote | 1-3 items: number (allowlisted) + label 2-6 words |
| S07 | press bar | optional | Borrow authority | only real press or institutions on record |
| S08 | buy box | yes | Close with the offer, what's included and the risk reversal | product_name; options 1-3 (plan or bundle) with price and anchor from the allowlist; includes 3-6 items; gifts only if real; cta 2-5 words; guarantee 6-15 words |
| S09 | reviews | optional | Short headlined reviews | 3-5 verbatim sourced reviews |
| S10 | faq | yes | Answer the last objections | 4-6 Q&A; answers 15-45 words |
| S11 | sticky cta | yes | Keep the claim button in reach | text 4-8 words (discount active); cta 2-4 words |

## Adaptation notes

No named rivals on record: compare against "typical [category]" with facts the brand can back, or drop the table. No author: drop the byline rather than invent one. A brand with one strong reason should not use this template.

## Watch-outs

Every button goes to one link with the discount already applied. Standalone (no site menu) beat on-store at The Perfect Jean. Review counts must match across the ad and the page (TPJ showed four different counts).
