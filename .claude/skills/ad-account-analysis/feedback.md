**2026-11-04, open test: which ads earn a place in the scaling campaign (a practitioner on X, after a talk with another; not yet tried on an account)**
- His rule: scaling campaigns take only ads whose first-click ROAS beats both what the platform
  reports and what the third-party attribution tool reports, and whose new-visitor share (his
  "NVP"; read as new-visitor percentage, check the term in the brand's attribution tool) beats the
  account average. Statics and bottom-of-funnel ads stay in the ABO testing campaign and are
  scaled there on third-party attribution, with a ROAS target 20% above the normal one. He says
  spend is at its highest and CPA is falling since he started (Stated, one practitioner, no
  numbers shown)
- Why it might hold: it is the same worry as the open test below and "Attribution settings" in
  `ad-account-analysis.md`. Statics skew BOF (the funnel-stage section there), and BOF ads collect
  credit from people who would have bought anyway, so they look best exactly where they add least
- The test: on the next account read with both platform and third-party attribution available,
  list the ads in the scaling campaigns with their first-click ROAS, platform ROAS, third-party
  ROAS and new-visitor share, and mark which would pass his rule. Note the statics and BOF ads
  among them. Log what share of scaling spend sits on ads that would fail it. Don't recommend
  moving budget from one account's read; that waits for a second brand

**2026-11-02, open test: a sign of credit that isn't incremental (Barry Hott on X, 22 Oct 2026; not yet tried on an account)**
- His claim: in the placement breakdown, when 1-day view conversions are as many as or more than
  1-day click conversions, much of that credit is probably not incremental. The typical case is
  ads shown to existing customers or recent visitors who would have bought anyway, optimised on
  7-day click, 1-day view. Optimising for incremental attribution instead shifts spend across
  users, placements and ads toward new sales (Stated, one practitioner)
- The test: on the next account read with breakdowns available, pull the placement breakdown with
  1-day click and 1-day view as separate columns. Note the placements where view ≥ click, whether
  they're retargeting-heavy, and how they compare with the store's own new-customer numbers. Log it
  here. Read it next to "Attribution settings" in `ad-account-analysis.md`, which already has his
  April 2026 view on incremental attribution
