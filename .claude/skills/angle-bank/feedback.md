# Feedback log

What we've observed building angle banks and reading gap matrices: which gaps turned into winners,
which thresholds misled, what the tags hid. A log, not a rulebook: new lessons land here with a date,
and once one has held on a second brand it moves into `SKILL.md` or
`brain-system/creative-strategy-context/angle-discovery.md`, leaving one line here saying where it
went. No brand details here; brand lessons go in the brain's `running-notes/feedback.md`.
