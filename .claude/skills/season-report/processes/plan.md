# From findings to this year's plan

The plan is the last section of the report and the reason it exists. It answers: when does each thing happen this year, what gets made, what gets tested, and what is ready before it's needed.

## 1. Move the dates

Move last year's dates by the number of days the sale moved, keeping the weekday (method and the worked 2026 table: `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/black-friday-and-q4-data.md`, "Moving the
calendar to this year"). Use the brand's own dates from the retro first; the cross-brand dates fill the gaps and are labelled as such.

## 2. The calendar

One table, this year's dates, a row per milestone, each with its source (the brand's own season, or cross-brand):

| Milestone | Typical source |
|---|---|
| Test new holiday ideas (cheap window ends) | Study 1: the week the brand's CPM started to climb |
| Sale creative made and live at low spend, no price claim | Study 3: when last year's winners first ran; study 4: a week before the peak at the least |
| Offer period starts (price claims allowed) | The brand's offer brief; the Danish 10-day rule |
| Peak week | Study 1 |
| Cyber Monday, if at all | Study 8 |
| December swap-in live | Study 9: the day the main sale ad stopped |
| Gift buyers peak; creative for them | Study 10 |
| Last order date before Christmas; gift cards | The brand's shipping cutoffs |
| Q1 creative launched | Study 1: the cheapest week |

## 3. What to make

- **Adapt first.** List last season's winners and this year's evergreen winners that can carry the deal (the adaptation ladder in `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/gifting-and-q4-creative.md`), from study 5 and the brand's current
  top spenders
- **The mix:** how many brand-new ideas, new takes and same-words ads, set from study 5 and `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/creative-volume.md`, with the numbers needed per week from study 2
- **Formats:** from studies 7, 12 and 13, where they pass the size rule; the cross-brand findings where they don't, labelled
- **For the audience shift:** creative for the buyer, if study 10 shows one

## 4. What to test

Each test: the question, the two versions, the date it starts, and the day-7 read it will be judged on. Take tests from findings that were * (too small to use), from where the brand broke a cross-brand
pattern, and from the open tests in `.claude/skills/offer-ads/feedback.md`.

## 5. The Danish rules, applied

Go through the calendar against `.claude/skills/offer-ads/offer-copy.md`, "The Danish price-marketing rules": the 10-day rule (state the full period from the start if longer), the 30-day lowest price (and its effect on
December), true urgency only. Mark each row that touches a price claim. Not legal advice; when in doubt, ask the brand.

## 6. Hand-offs

Name the next run for each part: `offer-ads` (with the calendar's dates in the offer brief), `static-ads` or `iterations` for the adapted winners, `scriptwriting` and `video-edits` for new takes,
`weekly-creative-report` to read the day-7 signals during the sale.
