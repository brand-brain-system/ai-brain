# Current-state rubric

Score each row 0-5 from evidence in the brain. One line of evidence per score, with its label
(`brain-system/system/evidence-and-labels.md`). No evidence: **Data-limited**, and the row's "what
would settle it" goes to `running-notes/missing-context.md`. Don't average the scores into one number;
the shape is the finding.

| Score | Means |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not done at all |
| 1 | Done once or by accident; nobody owns it |
| 2 | Exists, but basic or broken in ways the evidence shows |
| 3 | Works and is owned; obvious gaps remain |
| 4 | Good, measured, improved on a cadence |
| 5 | A strength rivals would copy; evidence that it drives results |

## Reach: strangers find the brand

| Row | Look at | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social creative | The newest ad-account read in `audits/`, `idea-bank/`, sprint retros | New concepts every week, winners iterated, more than one persona and format in the spend |
| Persona coverage | `persona-gap` Mode B output, `personas/personas-profile.md` | The top entry moments each have live ads |
| Search and AI answers | `search-visibility/` readouts | On the shortlist for the core topics in most runs; facts correct |
| Creators and organic | `creators/tracker.csv`, `sub-context-docs/organic-channels-inventory.md` | A pool that gets every brief; organic posts on a cadence with a learning loop |
| Earned and third-party | `sub-context-docs/reputation-analysis.md`, teardown source maps | Recent coverage, test results or retailer listings that the answers and buyers cite |

## Convert: visitors buy

| Row | Look at | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Landing pages and product pages | `cro-review` outputs, `sub-context-docs/website-and-product-audit.md` | Message match to the ads, proof near the buy button, tested versions |
| The offer | `strategy/offer-brief.md`, `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/offer-design.md` lever scores | No lever below 3; a real guarantee; true urgency only |
| Proof | `brand-lens.md` proof inventory | Recent reviews in volume, specific results, press or tests, all usable in ads |

## Repeat: buyers come back

| Row | Look at | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Consent and list | `lifecycle/consent-audit.md` | Valid consent texts, documented, growing list; the existing-customer exception set up right |
| Flows | `lifecycle/flow-map.md` | The minimum program live (`lifecycle-flows/flows.md`), each with a legal basis that holds |
| Repeat rate | Store data, `running-notes/success-definition.md` | Known, tracked by cohort, improving |

## Recommend: buyers bring others

| Row | Look at | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | Review platform counts over time, the review request flow | Every buyer asked, neutrally; review count rising month on month |
| Word of mouth | Post-purchase survey ("en ven eller familie" share), comments | Measured, and something gives customers a reason to share |

## Measure: the team knows what works

| Row | Look at | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Source of truth | `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/measurement-and-attribution.md` against what the team reports | Store as truth, platform numbers read as claims, MER and new-customer cost tracked |
| Survey and tests | `tool-map.md` (post-purchase survey route), open loops | A post-purchase survey running; at least one incrementality test a quarter on the biggest channel |
| Learning loop | `open-loops/`, `validations/`, retros | Hypotheses closed and written into the brain |

## Weight

Weigh each stage by the goal. A brand with plenty of first orders and a weak repeat rate puts Repeat
first even if Reach scores lower; a brand with no list puts Reach and the signup first. Say the
weighting and why in the plan.
