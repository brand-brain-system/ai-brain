# Feedback log

What we've observed in growth plans: which rubric rows were hard to score from a brain, which bets
held after 90 days, what the owners pushed back on. A log, not a rulebook: new lessons land here with
a date, and once confirmed on a second brand they move into `SKILL.md` or `rubric.md` (factory rule
6), leaving one line here saying where they went. Brand-specific lessons go in the brain's
`running-notes/feedback.md`.

## Open tests

- **31 Oct 2026, can a brain score every stage?** Most brains are built from the ad account and
  reviews, so Repeat and Measure will often be Data-limited on the first run. Note on the first brand
  how many rows were scorable, and whether the plan was still useful. If not, make
  `lifecycle-flows` (audit mode) a prerequisite
