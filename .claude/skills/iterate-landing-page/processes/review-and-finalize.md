# Review, revise, and finalize

## Review

Send the new page itself in its reviewable form, plus a full desktop screenshot. Summarize:

- the user's direction;
- what changed;
- what was intentionally preserved;
- open placeholders or evidence gaps;
- any baseline defect left untouched.

Do not publish a preview or use an artifact unless the reviewer asks. Their notes apply to the iteration, not the untouched baseline.

## Revise

Route each note to the relevant source, update `change-plan.md` when scope changes, rebuild, compare to the baseline, and rerun QA. Material new copy goes through the changed-copy table before the next build. Send the rebuilt page again with one line per change.

## Finalize

After approval, rerun desktop and phone QA and write `README.md` with:

- baseline source and capture date;
- original direction and final approved delta;
- exact files changed or produced;
- preserved systems and any intentional departures;
- copy, grounding, and voice receipts when applicable;
- final QA result and pre-existing defects kept out of scope;
- CTA destination, open placeholders, asset needs, and claim approvals still required;
- temporary preview details, if one was requested;
- implementation notes for the person putting the new version live.

Send the final page again. Do not launch it, attach a production domain, or change live routing without explicit authority.
