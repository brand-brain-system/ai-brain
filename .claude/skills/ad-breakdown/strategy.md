# Ad breakdown: strategy

How to pick the process from what the person hands over. Pick one, say it in one line ("This is a video ad from our
account, so I'm running the video breakdown."), then run it in full.

## What to check before deciding

- **Is it one ad or many?** "Break down this ad" is one. "Break down our winners", "what did our winners have in
  common", "which angles do we scale on" is a batch
- **Did it win or lose?** For our own ads, read it off the numbers against the brand's winner bar
  (`running-notes/success-definition.md`). The person's word ("this one flopped") is Stated until the numbers agree.
  For a competitor ad there is no win or lose, only "still running" (days live in the Library)
- **Video or static?** Decide once, the way `recreate` does:
  - motion, a voiceover or a script -> video
  - one image -> static
  - a carousel -> static, card by card, and say so
  - a video that is one still frame with text -> static, and say so
  - truly unclear -> ask one question and wait

## The processes

| Input | Process |
|---|---|
| One video ad (ours, a competitor's, a TikTok, a file, a link) that won or that we want to learn from | `processes/video-breakdown.md` |
| One static, carousel or still-frame video | `processes/static-breakdown.md` |
| The account's winners, "our top ads", "what do we scale on" | `processes/batch-winners.md` |
| One of our ads that failed, or a set of them | `processes/loser-breakdown.md` (it runs the video or static lens inside) |

## Tiebreakers

1. **The person names one ad and calls it a loser,** or its numbers miss the bar: loser-breakdown
2. **The person names one ad:** video or static breakdown, whatever its numbers. An ad that is "fine" still teaches
3. **The person asks about the account, not an ad:** batch-winners
4. **A competitor ad:** video or static breakdown, with the numbers section marked "Not our ad: no performance data".
   Never loser-breakdown on a competitor ad: we can't know it lost
5. **Two ads to compare** (a winner and a loser on the same angle or format): loser-breakdown, which already ends in
   that comparison

## When not to run it

- The person wants the brand's version of the ad: `recreate`
- The person wants variations now and doesn't need the read: `iterations`
- The person wants every ad a brand runs tagged and the gaps counted: `persona-gap` Mode B (with its short
  `ad-teardown.md` on the top ads)
- The person wants to know if the numbers can be trusted at all: `ad-account-analysis`, `measurement-check` first
