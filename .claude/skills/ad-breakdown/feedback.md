# Feedback log

What we've learned breaking down ads: which reads held, which misled, what the strategist caught that the breakdown
didn't. A log, not a rulebook: new lessons land here with a date, and once one has held on a second brand it moves into
`SKILL.md` or a process, leaving one line here saying where it went. No brand details here.
