# Ad breakdown: process index

- **video-breakdown**: one video ad, part by part: transcript, hook and opening, audience and awareness, story and
  structure, desire and emotion, belief and mechanism, offer and CTA, craft and native fit, formula with timestamps.
  Ends with the tag block and pattern check
- **static-breakdown**: one static or carousel: inventory, stop and eye flow, image, type and colour, audience and
  awareness, copy, desire and emotion, belief and mechanism, CTA, native fit, the spatial formula and its archetype.
  Ends with the tag block and pattern check
- **batch-winners**: the account's top winners (default top 10 by spend in 90 days that meet the brand's own bar), a
  light pass on each and a full pass on the top 3, then what the account actually scales on. Writes
  `angle-bank/ad-tags.csv`
- **loser-breakdown**: the same lens on an ad that failed, compared with a winner on the same angle or format

Every process asks for the strategist's read first (`SKILL.md`, step 2) and writes from `../breakdown-template.md`.
