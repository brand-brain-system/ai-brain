# Account playbook: strategy

Two choices before the first stage runs: where to enter, and how fast to go. Read the status page,
`tool-map.md`, `running-notes/brand-rules.md` and the newest file in `audits/` first. Write the
choice and the reason at the top of `running-notes/playbook-status.md`.

## Pick the entry point

| Situation | How you know | Enter at | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Fresh account** | Under about 90 days of spend, few purchases, or `measurement-check` marks the account or purchases cold | Stage 0 | Stage 1 is short and makes no benchmark comparison (the cold rule in `measurement-check`). Stage 2 may find no winners: mark it `skipped: no winners yet` and break down 3-5 of the longest-running rival ads instead, labelled proxies. Stages 3 and 4 carry the weight. Stage 5 is a discovery round |
| **B. Running for months, no system** | Months of spend, names that don't carry concept, angle or persona, no tag sheet, no gap map | Stage 0 | The full order. The naming step matters most: the old ads get tags from breakdowns and reads (`tag_source` = `breakdown` or `read`), not from their names. Expect the account to have scaled on one to three angles (Stated, from a practitioner's post); Stage 4 shows whether it did |
| **C. Brain, but no account access** | `tool-map.md` has no Ad account route | Stage 0, with the access ask as the first action | Run Stages 3 and 4 while waiting, and the public-only path for Stages 1 and 2 (`processes/stage-0-access.md`). When access arrives, re-run Stages 0-2 and `angle-bank` `map-account` before Stage 5 |
| **D. Mid-playbook resume** | The status page has stages marked `done` | The first stage not `done` or `skipped` | Check that each `done` stage's output file exists. Re-run an account read older than 30 days before Stage 5 uses it. Clear open stops first |
| **E. A system already in place** | The brand already names ads by angle and persona, keeps a tag sheet or a test setup | Stage 0 | Mark what exists `skipped: already in place (<file>)`, map it once (`creative-leaderboard` `naming-setup` for names), and go straight to the first missing piece |

If two fit, take the earlier stage. When unsure, ask the strategist one question through the
form: "Has this account been running for more than three months?"

## How fast to go, by spend

The spend bands and what each needs creatively are in
`brain-system/creative-strategy-context/creative-strategy-by-brand-size.md`; the number of ads a
month is in `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/creative-volume.md`. This table only sets the
pace of the playbook.

| Monthly Meta spend (rough band) | Pace | Stage 2 | Stage 7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Around $10k or less | The days in the stage table, no faster: there is little data to read | All ads that meet the winner definition, often only a few | If a week has too few results to rank, run the loop every two weeks and say so on the status page |
| Around $50k | The days in the stage table | `batch-winners` with its default set | Weekly |
| Around $1m or more | Run Stages 1-4 side by side in the first week; Stage 5 by day 10 | `batch-winners` with its default set, then a second batch on the top winners per persona | Weekly, from week 2 |

The bands are rough guides, as that doc says. Read the account's real testing capacity, and say
which band you used and why.
