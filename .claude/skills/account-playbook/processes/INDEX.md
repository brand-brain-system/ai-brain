# Account playbook: process index

One file per stage group. Each says the entry criteria, the inputs, what it calls (exact skill and
process names), the outputs in the brain, the stops and the exit check. The rules of the called
skills stay in those skills.

- **stage-0-access**: connect the ad account read-only, fill `tool-map.md`, run the tracking check
  first, confirm the winner definition, north star and naming. Holds the Day 0 checklist and the
  public-only path for when access is partial. Days 0-2
- **stage-1-2-read-and-breakdown**: the 90-day account read (structure, spend band, how many ads
  a month), then the breakdowns of the top winners with the strategist's read first, and what the
  account scaled on. Days 1-5
- **stage-3-4-angles**: mine the market in customers' own words, the research map, personas, own
  and rival ads against them; then the angle bank, the account mapped onto it and the gap matrix
  with the top 5 gaps. Days 3-12
- **stage-5-plan**: the roadmap, the gaps picked to test, the test setup, the sprint size, and the
  stop where the strategist approves the plan and the budget split. Days 10-14
- **stage-6-make**: per concept, shape first and voice second, then the making skills, creators
  when needed, the checks, naming, and the hand-off to the team. Weeks 2-4
- **stage-7-8-loops**: the weekly loop from week 3, the monthly loop, and the day-90 review

Run them in order unless `../strategy.md` picked another entry point. Stage 3 may start early.
