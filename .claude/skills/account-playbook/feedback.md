# account-playbook: feedback log

What we've observed running the playbook on real accounts: which stages took longer than planned,
which stops slowed things down, which order held. A log, not a rulebook. A lesson moves into
`SKILL.md` or a process file only after it has held on a second brand. No brand details here;
brand-specific notes go in the brain's `running-notes/feedback.md`.

## Log

**2026-11-07, open test: does the playbook find new winning angles fast?**
- The claim behind the order (a practitioner's post, Nov 2026): reading the last 90 days, tagging
  every winner by angle and persona, and setting that beside customer research points to the
  personas, angles and awareness levels that are missing; testing there gave 2-3x account growth within
  a few months (Stated, no data shown)
- The test, on the first two brands that run the playbook from Stage 0:
  - **Time from access to the first launch on a new angle,** in days (day 0 = the day access was
    granted, from the status page)
  - **How many of the Stage 4 top 5 gaps became winners by day 90,** by the brand's own winner
    definition. Write how many were launched as well, so "0 of 5" can be told apart from
    "0 of 1 launched"
- Also note which stage took longest and which stop waited longest
- Keep the order if the first launch lands inside 4 weeks and at least one gap wins by day 90 on
  both brands. If not, log what slowed it before changing the order
