# ai-ad-generation: feedback log

What we've observed on real runs. A log, not a rulebook. A lesson moves into SKILL.md or a process
only after it has held on a second brand.

## Open checks before the first real run

- `video-ai-drama` (added Oct 2026, from one vendor's demo): untested here. First run should record
  which image and video models held the cast's faces across all shots, how many frames and clips
  needed a fix and why, whether fixing the frame beat re-rolling the clip, draft-resolution-then-upscale
  cost against generating at full resolution, and how a spoken Danish line and the product name came
  out of the video model

## Runs
- **2026-11-08, open test: AI format by awareness, and non-realistic over realistic.** A
  practitioner's post says an all-AI ad became the top spender in an account, using a format picked
  by awareness level (strategy.md, "When the user asks which AI format to make") and characters that
  don't pretend to be real. Log, per brand: which format and awareness each AI ad had, whether it
  was built to look real or to look made, and its spend rank against the filmed ads. Holds when a
  non-realistic AI ad reaches the top 5 spenders on a second brand
