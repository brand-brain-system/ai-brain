# Offer copy

Offer-specific rules only. General copy rules: `static-ads/copy-guide.md`. Sizes and layout:
`static-ads/design-principles.md`. The copy itself is written in the brand's language.

## Ground rules
- The offer is concrete: "20 % på alt", "2 for 299 kr.", "Gratis [gave] ved køb over 500 kr." Never "kæmpe rabatter" or "vilde priser"
- One offer per ad. Two offers in one ad halve both. Say it once: a price line and a badge with the
  same offer is twice
- The season is a tag, not the headline. "Black Friday" sits small; the headline sells the product
  ("Your phone's perfect spot on the cart."), one bar carries the offer ("BUY 1, GET 1 FREE"), and the
  CTA speaks the category (the golf test brand, 6 Oct 2026)
- What, how much, until when. End date with weekday and date: "til og med mandag 30. november"
- Pair the offer with the best reason to buy the product. An offer on something the customer doesn't
  want doesn't sell. Cold traffic: product and problem first, the offer as the reason to buy now.
  Warm traffic (retargeting, customers): the offer first
- Q4 is gift season: say who it's for ("til ham der har alt", "gaven hun selv ville købe"), ideally in customers' own words
- Terms go in a small line at the bottom, still within the minimum sizes

Building or changing the offer itself (bundles, thresholds, gift with purchase, guarantees, which lever
to pull) is upstream of this file: `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/offer-design.md`. A guarantee
in an ad never presents the 14-day return right or the 2-year complaint right as a perk (markedsføringsloven,
Bilag 1 nr. 10). The same price rules apply to discounts in emails and SMS (`lifecycle-flows`).

## Danish number format
"299 kr.", "1.299 kr." (full stop as thousands separator), "20 %" (space before %), "30. november". Written exactly like this in the copy and prompt.

## The Danish price-marketing rules
Source: Forbrugerombudsmanden, [Priser og besparelsesudsagn](https://forbrugerombudsmanden.dk/alle-emner/forbud-mod-vildledning/priser-og-besparelsesudsagn) and [press release on Black Friday, 25 June 2026](https://forbrugerombudsmanden.dk/pressemeddelelser/2026/20260625-forbrugerombudsmanden-husk-reglerne-om-prismarkedsfoering-naar-i-planlaegger-jeres-black-friday-kampagner). Read 28 September 2026. Check for changes before a big campaign. Not legal advice.
- **Before-price = the lowest price in the last 30 days** before the reduction. Words like
  "Black Friday-pris", "tilbudspris", "spar 20 %" and "før/nu" signal a price reduction and may only
  be used when the saving is real against that price
- **An offer may normally be marketed for 10 days** (5 days for goods that expire or deteriorate
  quickly). It may run longer if the duration is stated at the start of the offer period, but never
  more than half the period the normal price applied. A Black Week from Friday 20 to Monday 30
  November 2026 is 11 days: state the full period in the ads from day one, or shorten it. An offer
  can't be extended past 10 days along the way
- **Campaigns affect the next campaign.** If an item sells for 400 kr. on Black Friday, 400 kr. is the
  lowest price for 30 days, and a Christmas discount in December is calculated from 400 kr. Plan
  Christmas variants for it
- **"Fra" prices** only if the item can actually be bought at that price
- **No artificial before-prices:** raising the price before a sale in order to cut it is illegal
- **Urgency and stock must be true.** "Sidste chance", "kun i dag" and "få på lager" only when true. If
  the ad says last day, the offer may not be extended afterwards
- **"Op til X %"** isn't covered by the two sources above. Use it only when X % really applies to
  items in the campaign, and ask the brand when in doubt

## Phases of a Black Friday campaign
| Phase | Purpose | Copy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser / early access | Build anticipation, collect sign-ups | When it starts, who gets it first. No price before it applies |
| Live | Convert | The concrete offer + the reason to buy |
| Last day | Final push | End date and time. Only if it really ends |
| Cyber Monday (optional) | Second wave, only if the brand has a reason | Its own offer, or part of a period stated from the start. Across brands few switched to it and Black Friday wording kept the money; a Black Week period that runs through the Monday usually covers it |
| December swap-in | Ready the day the sale ends | Gift angle and delivery deadline, made before the sale starts so it can replace the sale ads at once |
| Christmas / mellemdagssalg | Gifts, then sale | Gift angle, last order date for delivery before Christmas. Mind the 30-day rule after Black Friday |

Timing and what tends to win in the sale week (cross-brand data, Stated): `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/black-friday-and-q4-data.md`. Its short version for offer ads: the creative is made and live before the peak, at low spend, without a price claim until the offer period starts; in the sale week the deal goes on the creative, including on proven evergreen ads; statics with a deal keep up with video, so statics are a good home for the offer and for late additions during the sale.

## Avoid
Invented numbers, vague superlatives, "gratis" for something that requires a purchase without
naming the condition, and several offers in one ad.
