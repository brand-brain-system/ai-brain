# Danish rules for creator recruitment

What the skill follows, with the source for each rule. Labels as in the brains: **Verified** (read
in the primary source), **Stated** (a secondary source says it, not checked against the primary),
**Inferred** (our reasoning, with what it rests on). This is a working guide, not legal advice: for
a paid client or a big programme, have the employer's lawyer or a Danish influencer lawyer confirm.

Last checked: 2026-09-29.

## Outreach: the spam ban (markedsføringsloven § 10)

- **Verified.** The spam ban covers email, SMS and social media private messages (Facebook
  Messenger is named in a case), and it applies to messages to businesses as well as consumers.
  Source: Forbrugerombudsmanden, *Vejledning om spamforbuddet* (2021), chapter 5 and appendix 1.
- **Verified.** "Det vil som udgangspunkt heller ikke være markedsføring omfattet af spamforbuddet,
  hvis en virksomhed sender et tilbud om et samarbejde til en influencer." The same passage says a
  job posting sent to a candidate isn't marketing either. Source: same guidance, the list of examples
  of what counts as marketing (p. 17).
- **Inferred, from the same list.** The exemption is for an *offer of collaboration*. A message
  that also sells (a discount code, "see our new collection", a newsletter) is marketing and needs
  prior consent. The guidance treats a discount code to a former customer and an offer tied to a
  discount as marketing. So: keep the first message to the collaboration and nothing else.
- **Inferred.** "Som udgangspunkt" means "as a starting point": a borderline message is judged case
  by case. Staying strictly on the collaboration is what keeps us inside it.

## Creator data: GDPR

- **Stated.** Datatilsynet: data the person published themselves supports processing under
  legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)), and a closer relationship supports it more. Source: search
  summary of Datatilsynet guidance on direct marketing and when processing is allowed
  (datatilsynet.dk, "Hvornår må du behandle personoplysninger"). Not read in full.
- **Inferred, from GDPR itself.** Data we collect about a creator from their public profile (not
  from them) triggers the duty to inform (art. 14): who we are, the purpose, where we got the data,
  how long we keep it, and their right to object. The short line plus the privacy-policy link in
  the first message covers this, as long as the employer's privacy policy mentions creator
  recruitment. **Check that it does.**
- **Inferred, from GDPR's data minimisation and storage limitation (art. 5).** Store only what the
  collaboration needs (the tracker columns), and delete non-responders on a set schedule. The
  6 months in `creators/README.md` is our choice.
- **Inferred.** Scraping thousands of profiles "just in case" is hard to justify as necessary, which
  is one more reason for the shortlist approach.

## Marking: hidden advertising (markedsføringsloven § 6)

- **Stated.** Content the creator posts because of a payment *or a free product* must be marked as
  advertising. For adults, "reklame", "annonce", "betalt af" or "sponsoreret af" are clear enough;
  for content aimed at children and young people, only "reklame" and "betalt af". The marking must
  come first and be easy to see, not hidden among hashtags. Source: Forbrugerombudsmanden,
  *Vejledning om reklameidentifikation* and "Forbud mod skjult reklame" (forbrugerombudsmanden.dk),
  via search summaries. Read the guidance before the first campaign.
- **Stated.** The brand is responsible too, not just the creator: there are cases where influencers
  were found in breach even without a formal agreement. Source: Forbrugerombudsmanden case 24/05922
  and 24/06344 (forbrugerombudsmanden.dk). Put the marking requirement in every collaboration brief.
- **Inferred.** Content that only runs as an ad from the brand's own account is already an ad, so
  the marking rule is about the creator's own posts. Whitelisted ads run through the creator's
  account: show them with the platform's paid-partnership label.

## Contracts and minors

- **Inferred, from Danish guardianship law (værgemålsloven).** People under 18 can't enter binding
  contracts on their own, so the guardian signs. The skill stops and flags any creator who looks
  under 18. Confirm with a lawyer before working with minors at all.
