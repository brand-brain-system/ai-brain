# Feedback log

What we've observed in email and SMS work: which flows paid, which timings and subjects worked, what a
platform or a lawyer pushed back on. A log, not a rulebook: new lessons land here with a date, and once
confirmed on a second brand they move into `SKILL.md` or `flows.md` (factory rule 6), leaving one line
here saying where they went. Brand-specific lessons go in the brain's `running-notes/feedback.md`.

## Open tests

- **31 Oct 2026, flow holdout.** Platforms credit a flow with every order placed within days of an
  open or click, so a welcome or cart flow looks better than it is. On the first brand with enough
  volume, hold 10 % out of one flow for 4-6 weeks and compare order rates. Log the gap between the
  platform's attributed revenue and the measured lift. Method: `brain-system/creative-strategy-context/measurement-and-attribution.md`
- **31 Oct 2026, discount timing in cart reminders.** `flows.md` puts any discount in the last
  reminder, not the first (Corey Haines' `sms` skill, Stated). Test on the first brand that allows a
  cart discount: discount in reminder 3 vs no discount at all
