Ad Account Playbook

What we do, in order, from the day a new brand gives us read access to its Meta ad account until the account scales on more than one or two angles. Every step is a skill in the factory; every output is a file in the brand's own brain repo on GitHub.

The 90 days

Nine stages, from access to the monthly loop

    Every week, from week 3

    The feedback loop

    1. Launch, named. Every ad carries its angle, persona, awareness, format, voice and shape in its name. The team launches; Claude never does.
    2. Leaderboard. creative-leaderboard ranks the week by angle, persona, format and voice, with hit rate. The next batch starts from what won, not from what someone liked.
    3. Breakdown. ad-breakdown on the best and the worst. The strategist writes a guess first; the breakdown shows what they missed.
    4. Angle bank. Statuses move: idea, briefed, live, won, lost. Tags land in angle-bank/ad-tags.csv.
    5. Gap matrix. Angles against awareness levels. An empty cell with strong customer evidence is a message the buyers have never heard.
    6. Brief and make. Shape first, voice second. A new angle goes on a proven format; a proven angle goes into new formats.
    7. Policy check. ad-policy-check is the last gate before the team launches.
