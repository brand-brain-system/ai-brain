# Skills catalog

Every skill in `.claude/skills/`: what it does, what it deliberately doesn't, and how far it has been tested. **Update the row in the same change as the skill.** Status words: **Tested** (run on
real or test-brand work with logged feedback), **Written** (complete, never run on real work), **Parts tested** (scripts or one mode run).

Status as of release v1. Skill names changed from the working setup: `static-ads` (was `statiske-annoncer`), `offer-ads` (`tilbudsannoncer`), `video-edits` (`videoklip`), `song-ads` (`musikannoncer`),
`motion-ads` (`motion-annoncer`), `brief-check` (`brief-tjek`), `creator-recruitment` (`creator-rekruttering`), `edit-training` (`klip-traening`).

| Skill | Does | Deliberately doesn't | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `weekly-brain-report` | The weekly read as one clean shareable page: three things that matter, what's working and dying, competitors' moves and gaps, customers' exact words, openings (organic and affinity), what to make next, optional blind judging. A template: every section optional, every line labelled, gaps named. Works from public data, sharper with an account export. `render_weekly.py` renders HTML and PDF | Invent numbers; call a winner from the ad library; send anything itself | Written, example rendered |
| `brand-dna` | Researches a brand's identity from public sources (design credits, fonts and hex from the CSS, packaging, photography, ads, positioning, 2-3 rivals), writes each fact to its home in the brain with a label, exports a one-page Brand DNA with a 50-75 word image-prompt modifier | Invent fonts, hex, agencies or numbers; rank ads from the Ad Library | Tested (one test brand) |
| `persona-gap` | **Mode A:** trigger-based personas from real customer language, entry moments ranked by evidence, every field labelled, audited by an agent that didn't write them. **Mode B:** tags every live ad by persona, awareness, funnel, message, format; gap tables and a diagnosis | Use private data; take shares from keyword-searched pulls; derive funnel stage from awareness (it is read from delivery on our account, `x` on public ads) | Tested, both modes |
| `research-map` | Customer research as one ranked, clickable page: quotes saved once with an open-original link, grouped by layer (situation, symptom, belief, desire, product experience), each group counted by different people in the unweighted pull; click a desire to light up what links to it. Feeds persona-gap A3 | Group automatically (a person approves the groups first), fetch posts, count the same person twice, or count searched pulls | Parts tested (script on a synthetic file, page checked in a browser; not on real research) |
| `static-ads` | Static ads in volume: templates from the swipe library with fit rules, the human's own angle first, copy from customer language, copy checks, a STOP for copy approval, image generation with an HTML text layer, checks on every image, a review grid, a STOP for the pick, variants. Modes: full run, dry run, prompt pack, calibration | Invent claims, numbers, reviews or badges; imitate review-platform styling | Tested (2 runs + a prompt-pack dry run) |
| `offer-ads` | Offer statics (Black Friday, Christmas, sales) from an approved offer brief with real prices; Danish price-marketing rules; 74 Q4 offer templates | Show "spar" or before/now prices without a documented 30-day lowest price; invent any number | Written |
| `season-report` | Last year's sale season from the brand's own Meta export, run as the cross-brand Q4 study on one brand (CPM by week, volume and hit rate, sale ads on and off, day-7 read, new ideas vs new takes, deals, what won the sale week, audience shift, gifting, statics vs video, openers), each result beside the cross-brand number; a report and a separate methods file in a fixed form; this year's dated plan under the Danish price rules. Public-only mode without an export | Make ads; set prices or dates; compare ROAS with other brands; call a small group a finding | Written |
| `video-edits` | Raw footage to ads: transcribe, trimmed cut for approval, B-roll and styled captions, every hook × length rendered with ffmpeg. Modes: standard, one-shot, iteration, reference style, batch, mashup, AI talking head. Shared frame check | Change what anyone said; drive editing software; heavy effects | Tested once (English test footage); cut-check tested on synthetic audio |
| `motion-ads` | An approved static becomes a 6-10 s motion ad rendered from code (a `seek(t)` page, headless Chrome, ffmpeg), in 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 from one layout; also transparent hook, end and offer cards for video work | Write new copy; redraw the product; synthesise music; spend on generation | Parts tested (engine on a placeholder) |
| `song-ads` | An animated story ad carried by an original AI song: story spine, lyrics and shot plan, song, character sheets, storyboards, cheap drafts, 1080p for approved takes, assembly. Six stops, price before every spend | Real-customer stories, competitor scripts, artist names | Written |
| `new-landing-page` | One landing page per run from five proven structures and a swipe file: human picks the structure and approves the copy; self-contained HTML, desktop and phone checks, QA, handoff list | Pick the template; invent proof; reuse a reference's words or images; publish | Parts tested (scripts) |
| `iterate-landing-page`, `cro-review`, `page-qa`, `extract-page-template` | A new version of an existing page; a CRO teardown; a functional QA at desktop and phone widths; a new structure for the library | Submit forms, follow CTAs, launch | Written (`page-qa` script tested) |
| `brief-check` | Checks raw footage for missing must-have shots and finished ads for spelling, claims and brief delivery; drafts comments and messages | Post, send or move anything without approval; judge looks | Written |
| `creator-recruitment` | A creator shortlist with a fit reason each, outreach drafts within the Danish spam, GDPR and marking rules, a pipeline tracker, an optional reply loop | Mass-scrape or mass-message; agree rates | Written |
| `lifecycle-flows` | The email and SMS program for the Danish market: consent audit of every capture point, flow map with each flow's legal basis, ranked gaps, flows written from customer language and checked against the spam ban, the existing-customer exception and the price rules; signup capture; results with the platform's numbers read as claims. Legal file read from the primary guidance | Activate a flow, send or schedule anything, edit a list or consent fields; offer a gift for a signup or a reward for a review | Written (legal file checked against Forbrugerombudsmanden's 2021 guidance; not run on a brand) |
| `ai-search` | How AI assistants and search recommend the brand in Danish: a versioned question portfolio, 3 runs per assistant in the browser, `tally.py` for rates with their n and the cited domains, wrong facts, site checks (bots, readable facts, structured data, feed), a fix list routed to skills or people | Post or review anywhere, change the site, write pages "for AI", report one run as a ranking | Parts tested (`tally.py` on a synthetic log; not run on a brand) |
| `competitor-teardown` | How a rival grew: `teardown.py` (Shopify catalog with launch dates and sale share, tool stack and homepage changes by month from web archives, Denmark-set ad library links), the funnel from outside, with approval their emails and cart reminders from the inside for 30-60 days, a dated timeline, a gap table and plays routed to our skills | Sign up, buy or contact anyone without a yes; work around blocks; copy their words or a practice that breaks Danish rules | Parts tested (script on a public store, short window; not a full teardown) |
| `growth-plan` | The whole-funnel plan: rubric scores for reach, convert, repeat, recommend and measure from the brain, unit economics from the brand's own numbers, three bets, a 90-day roadmap and 12-month outlook over the season calendar, every move routed to a skill with an indicator and a kill criterion, plays to skip | Invent numbers, commit spend, override the strategic roadmap | Written |
| `ad-policy-check` | The last gate before a paid ad launches: copy, image text, people and landing page against the platform's own rules (fetched live from Meta's Transparency Center) and the Danish rules in their homes; personal attributes, before/after, fake interface, branded content, the AI label; stop, fix or note per ad with the rule quoted and an honest rewrite; a disapproval mode that finds the line that caused it; a stop on ads run from a made-up Page or person. Called by every skill that makes paid ads and by `brief-check` | Submit, launch, appeal or edit in the ad account; soften a claim to keep it; call an ad safe | Written |
| `edit-training` | Turns narrated edits and old review comments into edit rules and a review rubric; calibrates AI feedback against humans; back-tests `video-edits` on shipped ads and counts versions to approval | Train on the calibration set | Written |
| `account-playbook` | The front door once a brand gives us its Meta ad account: nine stages from read-only access and a tracking check, through the 90-day read, winner breakdowns, market mining, the angle bank and gap matrix, the test plan and making the ads, to the weekly and monthly loops. Each stage names its skill, output file, stop and exit check; progress in `running-notes/playbook-status.md`. Visual: `system/workflow-map.html` | Do the analysis or writing itself; launch, pay or change anything in the account; ask for more than `ads_read` | Written |
| `ad-breakdown` | One ad (video or static, ours or a rival's) or a batch of the account's winners, read part by part: hook, awareness and sophistication, story, desire and LF8, valence and intensity, belief and mechanism, NESB, offer, craft and native fit, formula. The strategist's own read first, a "your read vs the breakdown" section last, the shared tag block feeding `angle-bank/ad-tags.csv`. Loser mode | Recreate or iterate (routes to `recreate`, `iterations`); call a winner without the brand's bar; edit the account | Written |
| `angle-bank` | Net-new angles: customer words verbatim into one-line angles tagged by persona and awareness, the last 90 days of ads tagged by angle, persona and awareness, the share of winner spend per angle, and `gap_matrix.py` (angles and personas x awareness, won/live/lost/never tried, empty cells ranked by evidence) ending in five test proposals for the idea bank | Swipe rivals' winners as angles; rank a cell on emptiness alone; brief before the strategist picks | Parts tested (script on synthetic data; not run on a brand) |
| `creative-leaderboard` | The weekly internal ranking by angle, persona, awareness, format, voice, shape and concept, read from ad names (`system/ad-naming-convention.md`, or the brand's own map in `running-notes/ad-naming.md`), with hit rate against the brand's winner bar; `leaderboard.py` (Danish exports too), `ad_name.py`, name audit, naming setup with a suggested-names CSV; fills `angle-bank/ad-tags.csv` | Rename, pause or launch anything in the account; invent the winner bar; replace `weekly-creative-report` | Parts tested (scripts on synthetic data) |
| `improve-factory` (factory only, not synced to brains) | Turns handed-in practitioner material into a factory release: loads `scripts/factory-map.py` instead of re-exploring, splits sources into claims, maps coverage, picks one home per item (rule 6 for new rules), builds with parallel agents from a shared contract, wires and releases vN | Store sources in the repo or paste them unchanged; run inside a brain; tag without asking | Written (shaped on the v20 run) |
