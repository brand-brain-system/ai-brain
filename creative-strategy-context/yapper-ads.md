# Yapper ads that find new angles

**Source:** a whiteboard photo handed in by the team on 5 Nov 2026 ("How to write Yapper ads that find new winning angles"). Author unknown: ask me.
**Users:** the whole team, all brands. Read by `scriptwriting` and `creator-briefs.md` when the ad is a yapper ad.
**Status:** *Stated* (one source, no data shown). Not yet tested on our accounts. Nothing added beyond the board except the pointers to our own docs.

A yapper ad is one person talking to camera and telling a story. It works when it feels like a friend telling a story, not a brand selling one.

The idea that makes it an angle machine: **new villain + same product = new angle.** You keep the product and the proof, and change who or what made the buyer feel the pain.

## The six steps

### 1. Mine their words first
Before writing anything, collect the customer's own phrases and copy them exactly. Where to look:
- the brand's own 3-4 star reviews (honest, with a "but")
- competitors' 1-2 star reviews (the pain the rival left behind)
- Reddit threads, TikTok comments
- support tickets and customer surveys

How to pull and sort them: `customer-review-mining-method.md`, and `research-map` in the brain when there is a lot.

### 2. Find the villain moment
Who or what made them feel it? A shop assistant, a doctor, a product that made it worse. Each different villain is a different angle, so list every villain the words in step 1 name.
The same idea counted across 100 winning scripts (the enemy, in 84 of them): `winning-script-patterns.md`.

### 3. Hook with the pain on screen
Frame 1 shows the problem, has movement, and opens a loop. It must make sense on mute. The hook craft itself lives in `hooks.md` and `hook-psychology.md`; the first-frame rule in `killer-performance-ads.md` (Ugly Ads).

### 4. Tell the story like a friend
Villain, then frustration, then discovery. Every line earns the next one. No rambling. How spoken lines should sound: `spoken-script-voice.md`.

### 5. Drop the product in as the discovery
Never in frame 1. It arrives after the villain, as the answer. Test: the story should still work with the product taken out.

### 6. Prove it, then close the loop
- Demo it on camera or show the result
- One line on how it works (the mechanism)
- Kill one objection
- Back to the villain, then the offer

Offers in Danish ads follow the price rules in `offer-design.md` and the brand's compliance file.

## The rules for the shoot

- **One take, minimal edits.**
- **Cast the buyer** (or someone they trust), not a model. Who can yap and how to find them: `creator-briefs.md`.
- **Bullet points, not a script.** The creator gets talking points. Our own rule on top: the hook is still written word for word (`creator-briefs.md`, "If you script one thing, script the hook"). Regulated categories still get a script (same doc).
- **Film where they'd film a friend:** car, bathroom, kitchen.

## What a yapper brief from us contains

1. The villain, named, with the customer quote it came from
2. The hook, word for word, with the frame-1 shot (setting, what moves, the open loop)
3. Bullets for the story: villain, frustration, discovery
4. The product moment and the proof shot
5. The mechanism line and the one objection to kill
6. The close: back to the villain, then the offer
7. Setting and who to cast

To make more angles, keep 4-6 and write a new 1-3 for each villain from step 2.

## What the board leaves open
- No numbers on how long the ad runs or how many villains to test per round
- "Something they trust" is not defined further; for health claims and experts the Danish rules in the brand's compliance file decide
- An AI person speaking as a customer is not a yapper ad under our guardrails (`legal-ai-ugc.md`)
