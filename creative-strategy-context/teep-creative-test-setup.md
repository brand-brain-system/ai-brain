# Creative test setup: T-E-E-P (Sarah Levinger)

**Source:** LinkedIn post by Sarah Levinger (Tether Insights), about 30 Oct 2026 (shown as "6d" on 5 Nov 2026). Link: unknown: ask me.
**Users:** the whole team, all brands.
**Status:** *Stated* (one practitioner, no data shown). Not yet tested on our accounts. Nothing added beyond the post.

What T-E-E-P (Trigger, Exploration, Evaluation, Purchase) means as a buying phase is defined in `emotional-delivery-and-timing.md`. This doc is only about her test setup.

## 1. Account setup

- Consolidate the account and start with a simple structure.
- 1 campaign with 4 adsets, 1 for each T-E-E-P category:
  - 1 Trigger adset
  - 1 Exploration adset
  - 1 Evaluation adset
  - 1 Purchase adset

## 2. Ads per test

- 3 new ads per new test, or up to 10 with a larger budget.

| Ads in the test | Video (UGC or AI ad) | Static | Last slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 carousel |
| 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 GIF or carousel |

## 3. Trigger ads: one micro-when

- Don't worry about avatars or "who" the ads bring in.
- Pick 1 "micro-when" to cover, and make all the ads speak to it.

Her example, for Opopop:

- The "when": "I hate it when I sit down to watch a movie and there's no good snacks."
- Video (UGC or AI ad): Snack-AI-Country-Song
- Static: The Day I Decided to Trash All My Snacks
- Carousel: 7 Bad Snacks I Ate on The Couch This Week (So You Don't Have To)

## How the concepts are chosen: customer data

| Signal in the data | What she does |
|---|---|
| AI-country ads are trending in Trendtrack | Tries that |
| Reddit has many new threads about people clearing out their snack drawers | Tries that |
| Reviews are full of people listing the other snacks they tried before hers | Runs that |

## 4. Weed out, then scale horizontally

- Weed out anything that isn't hitting.
- Scale anything that is hitting HORIZONTALLY.

Her example: if the carousel "7 Bad Snacks I Ate on The Couch This Week (So You Don't Have To)" crushed, the next concepts are:

- AI video: The 7 Bad Snacks Gang (long form storytelling)
- Static: There's No Worse Snacks Than These 7 (Prove Us Wrong)
- GIF: 7 Worst Snacks: Temple Run On HARD MODE

Her closing point: creative strategy is a game of combinations. The simpler the setup at the top, the more creative you can be at the bottom.
