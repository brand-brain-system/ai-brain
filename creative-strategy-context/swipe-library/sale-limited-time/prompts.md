## From the Adcrate playbook

### #37 · Hero statement + icon bar + offer burst
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-37-offer-burst.png`

Prompt: A promotional hero statement ad on a [BACKGROUND like dark charcoal] background. Top 12%:
a white or light banner with a massive bold [DARK COLOR] uppercase sans-serif headline "[2-3 WORD
STATEMENT]" ending with a full stop for punch. Upper left of the product area: a [BRIGHT ACCENT
COLOR like lime] comic-style starburst badge, rotated slightly, reading "[OFFER like GET UP TO 40%
OFF]" in bold black. Center: [PERSON'S HAND] gripping [OUR PRODUCT] from above, lifting it out of
its [PACKAGING like retail box] below. Product label and branding clearly visible. Moody, slightly
dramatic lighting. Bottom 20%: three evenly spaced icon-and-text benefit columns on a
semi-transparent dark strip: "[ICON 1 + LABEL]" | "[ICON 2 + LABEL]" | "[ICON 3 + LABEL]", icons
as simple line art in circles with [ACCENT COLOR] highlights. Very bottom: a full-width [BRIGHT
ACCENT COLOR] banner with bold dark text "[PROMO like BLACK FRIDAY SPECIAL]".

Must be sourced: the discount and promo (live offer, dated); the three benefit labels (brand
materials). The headline can't make a health or weight-loss claim the brand doesn't make.
