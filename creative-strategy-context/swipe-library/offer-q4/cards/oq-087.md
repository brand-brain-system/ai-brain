**Layout forensics.** A tall 9:16 frame runs a flat dark-chestnut field the full height, with a small cream serif wordmark centered at the top and a three-word headline beneath it where the final word steps up in size, the same escalating-word device as its sibling but resolving on the last word instead of the middle one. A dusty-rose banner spans the full width naming the bundled SKU in small caps. Directly below, the price zone runs a list figure alongside a parenthetical bundle value in the same bold cream serif — a price-anchor move rather than a strikethrough, letting the reader do the subtraction themselves. An arch-shaped rose badge to the side states the savings figure outright, with a line of tiny italic fine print tucked beneath it. The product is a genuine duo — a pot and a pan, each with its own utensil, staged diagonally with a soft cast shadow reinforcing depth on an otherwise flat field. Eye order: the escalating headline first, the price-anchor figure second, the savings badge third, the SKU banner fourth, the product last, closing the frame at the bottom of a long vertical scroll.

**The story this ad tells.** This ad is selling a bundle, not a single item, and every zone above the product is built to make the bundle math legible: what it's called, what it costs, what that number is worth against, and exactly how much that saves. The two utensils staged with the cookware are a small but real congruency detail — they signal the set is meant to be used together, not just packaged together.

**Copy skeleton.**
- headline — announces the sale, escalating emphasis on the final word, 3 words
- subhead (SKU banner) — names the bundled product, 4 words
- price — list value plus bundle value, number-led, 3 words
- badge — states the savings figure, number-led, 3 words
- disclaimer — qualifies the savings claim, fine print, 5 words

**Adaptation notes.** This layout needs a genuine two-piece (or more) bundle to earn the "duo" staging and the price-anchor math; a single-SKU brand should drop to the sibling strikethrough template instead of forcing a fake bundle value into this one. The savings badge and its fine-print qualifier travel together — never show a savings figure without the disclaimer that explains what it's measured against, since an unqualified savings number is exactly the kind of unsupported figure the brand's claims discipline flags. The utensils are a nice-to-have congruency touch, not structural; the layout holds without them if the brand's product doesn't ship with accessories.
