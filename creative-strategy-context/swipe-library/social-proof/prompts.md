materials, with source and date, or they're cut.

## From the Adcrate playbook

### #6 · The trust stack
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-06-trust-stack.png`

Prompt: A social proof ad on [BACKGROUND like warm cream]. Top: "[HEADLINE like Join 1,000,000+
Members]" in bold [BRAND COLOR]. Five filled stars with "Rated [X] out of 5". Center: [OUR PRODUCT]
at 50mm f/4. Below: a frosted white card with a five-star rating, "[REVIEW TITLE]", "[2-3 SENTENCE
REVIEW]", "[ATTRIBUTION]" in italic. Below the card: "As Featured In" with [NUMBER] grayscale
publication logos. Brand logo bottom right.

Must be sourced: the member count, the rating, the review, and every publication (only outlets
that actually covered the brand; upload their real logos, don't let the model draw them). Drop the
"As Featured In" row if there's no real press.

### #10 · Press / editorial
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-10-press-editorial.jpg`

Prompt: A press ad on an off-white linen background. Top: "As Featured In" in small [BRAND COLOR]
uppercase wide-tracked text. Below: [NUMBER] grayscale publication logos. Center: an italic serif
pull-quote in [BRAND COLOR]: "[PRESS QUOTE]" with attribution. Lower third: [OUR PRODUCT] at 85mm
f/2.8, soft side light. Brand logo bottom left. Generous white space, full-page magazine energy.

Must be sourced: the press quote verbatim with its outlet, and every logo. Without real press this
template doesn't apply.

### #15 · Social comment screenshot + product
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-15-comment-screenshot.png`

Prompt: A clean white background. Top: an oversized bold black sans-serif hook headline "[HOOK
HEADLINE]" with [EMOJI] at the end. Center: a social media comment card with a light gray
rounded-rectangle background: a small circular avatar top left, bold "[NAME]", and the comment in
regular sans-serif: "[COMMENT, 3-4 sentences, conversational]". A small gray timestamp "[TIMESTAMP
like 2d]" below. Bottom center: [OUR PRODUCT] at a slight angle on white, soft studio light. No
logo, no stars. It should look like someone screenshotted a real comment and dropped the product
photo below it.

Must be sourced: the comment and name (a real comment, with permission for live use).

### #26 · Stat callout (data-driven lifestyle)
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-26-stat-callout.png`

Prompt: Top 50%: lifestyle product photography, [SCENE like hands holding the product] on a
[MOOD like warm, soft-focus] background, packaging visible. Middle: brand logo centered with thin
horizontal rules on either side. Bottom 50%: a dark gradient overlay fading from transparent to
[DARK COLOR]. Large bold uppercase sans-serif text: "[STAT HEADLINE like AFTER SWITCHING TO BRAND,
X% OF USERS RESULT]", with key result phrases in [ACCENT COLOR].

Must be sourced: every percentage, from a study or survey the brand published, with the source.
The statistic is the whole ad, so without one this template doesn't apply.

### #33 · News article screenshot
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-33-news-article.png`
Use only when the brand has real coverage. Upload the real masthead as a reference image rather
than letting the model draw it, and clear its use with the brand.

Prompt: A static ad designed to look like a screenshot of an online news article. Top 25%: white
background with the masthead of [PUBLICATION, the outlet that actually ran the article]. A thin
gray rule. Small gray "[SECTION LABEL]". Bold black serif headline spanning the width: "[THE
ARTICLE'S REAL HEADLINE, verbatim]". Bottom 60%: two side-by-side casual phone photos of [PEOPLE]
each holding [OUR PRODUCT], one in daylight, one in warm indoor evening light, looking like
customer photos rather than studio shots. No logo, no CTA.

Must be sourced: the article (outlet, headline, date, URL). The playbook version invents a
headline, a price and a review count on a real masthead; we don't.
