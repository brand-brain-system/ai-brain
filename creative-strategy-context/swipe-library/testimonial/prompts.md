# Testimonial: prompts

Rules for building the final prompt: `../production.md`. In this category, every quote is a
real review, verbatim, with the reviewer's name as it appears in the source. No logo on anything
meant to look like a real review. Review-platform details (verified badges, helpfulness counts,
flags) only if they match the real source.

## From the Adcrate playbook

### #3 · Testimonial in a real setting
Suggested ratio: 9:16
Example image: `pb-03-testimonial.png`

Prompt: A testimonial ad set in [SETTING like bright bathroom / kitchen] with warm natural light.
[OUR PRODUCT] on [SURFACE], slightly out of focus. Overlaid: large bold white sans-serif "[SHORT
HEADLINE]". Below: "[FULL QUOTE, 2-3 sentences]. [NAME], [CREDENTIAL]." Five filled [BRAND COLOR]
stars. Brand logo bottom right in white. Shot on 35mm f/2.0.

Must be sourced: quote, name, credential, star rating.

### #9 · Negative marketing (bait and switch)
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-09-negative-bait.png`

Prompt: Background is a close-up of [OUR PRODUCT], slightly blurred. Center: a white
rounded-rectangle review card. Gray user icon, "[NAME]", [STAR RATING as in the source review],
bold text: "[BAIT: a line from a real review that sounds negative but is positive]". Bottom: bold
white sans-serif "[PUNCHLINE like THE REVIEWS ARE IN.]".

Must be sourced: the bait line, name and rating come from one real review. The original playbook
pairs a one-star rating with a rave; only do that if the real review was one star. Our
testimonial-05 is the same pattern.

### #11 · Pull-quote review card
Suggested ratio: 1:1 or 4:5
Example image: `pb-11-pull-quote-card.png`

Prompt: A review-driven ad with a solid [BRAND COLOR with hex, soft and muted works best] background
filling the image. Top half: large bold italic white serif with curly quotation marks: "[PULL-QUOTE,
the most emotional 4-8 word phrase from the review]". Below: five large filled gold stars. Bottom
left, overlapping the background: a white rounded review card with a subtle shadow: a small gray
circular default avatar, "[FIRST NAME + LAST INITIAL]" in bold dark sans-serif, then "[SOURCE
LABEL, only if true, like Verified buyer]" in small text. Below: the review body in dark
sans-serif, 4-6 lines, trailing off mid-sentence and ending with "...Read more" in bold [BRAND
COLOR]. Bottom right, overlapping card and background: [OUR PRODUCT, full packaging description]
angled slightly toward the viewer with a soft shadow.

Must be sourced: pull-quote, body text (a real review, cut at a natural point), name, star rating.
Drop the playbook's helpfulness count and flag unless they're real.

### #16 · Curiosity-gap hook quote
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-16-hook-quote.png`

Prompt: A clean white background. Top center: large [ACCENT COLOR] opening quotation marks. Below:
a mixed-weight headline in black: a first line in italic serif "[SETUP]", two lines in enormous
heavy bold all-caps sans-serif "[BAIT PHRASE]", then a smaller sentence-case line "[REVEAL]".
Closing quotation marks and "[ATTRIBUTION like - Erin D.]". Bottom left third: [OUR PRODUCT] at a
slight angle with [DETAILS like capsules scattered nearby], and to its left a [TRUST BADGE, only if
the brand offers it, like 60-day guarantee seal]. Bottom right third: five filled [ACCENT COLOR]
stars and "[REVIEW COUNT] 5-Star Reviews". Bottom edge: small disclaimer "[DISCLAIMER if needed]".

Must be sourced: the whole quote (setup, bait, reveal) from one real review; attribution; review
count; guarantee terms.

### #17 · Verified review card on color
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-17-verified-review-card.png`

Prompt: A solid [BRAND COLOR] background. Top: large bold white serif or semi-bold sans-serif
pull-quote "[QUOTE]" in quotation marks. Below: five large filled gold stars. Center left: a white
rounded review card with a subtle shadow: gray circular avatar, bold "[REVIEWER NAME]", "[SOURCE
LABEL, only if true]" in [BRAND COLOR], then 3-4 sentences of review text in regular dark text. At
the bottom of the card: "...Read more" in blue. Right side, overlapping the card edge: [OUR
PRODUCT] at a slight angle, soft studio light, gentle shadow. No brand logo.

Must be sourced: quote, review text, name, rating.

### #19 · Highlighted / annotated testimonial
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-19-highlighted-review.png`

Prompt: A clean white background. Top left: a circular customer headshot of [PERSON DESCRIPTION].
Beside it: bold "[REVIEWER NAME]". Below: a long customer quote in large regular black sans-serif
spanning most of the frame: "[FULL QUOTE, 3-5 sentences]". Key phrases have [HIGHLIGHT COLOR like
neon yellow] rectangular highlighter fills behind the text: "[PHRASE 1]", "[PHRASE 2]". Bottom
right: [OUR PRODUCT] at a slight angle, partly cropped at the bottom edge. To its left: a circular
[TRUST BADGE, only if real] seal. Brand logo small, bottom left.

Must be sourced: quote and name. The headshot is a generated stand-in for a real reviewer, so in
live ads use a real customer photo with consent, or drop the headshot.

### #24 · Product + comment callout
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-24-comment-callout.png`

Prompt: Top 55%: [OUR PRODUCT] centered on a clean white background, studio-lit, 85mm f/2.8, soft
shadow, [DETAIL showing use like lid slightly open], a few [LOOSE UNITS] spilling at the base.
Bottom 45%: a realistic Facebook-style comment card: small circular profile photo of [PERSON
DESCRIPTION], bold "[NAME]", comment text "[COMMENT, 2-3 sentences]", "[TIMEFRAME like 4w] · Like ·
Reply" in gray, reaction emojis with "[COUNT]". Should look like an organic screenshot.

Must be sourced: the comment must be a real comment on the brand's own post, with name and
reaction count as they appear. The playbook calls this "faux social proof"; we don't make it faux.

### #38 · UGC lifestyle + product + review card (split)
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-38-ugc-review-split.png`

Prompt: A vertical split. Left 55%: a casual UGC-style photo of [PERSON] enjoying the product in
context: [ACTION]. Natural light, warm, iPhone-quality; the person looks genuinely happy, not
posed. Right 45%: solid [PRIMARY BRAND COLOR] background with small [ACCENT COLOR] sparkle
accents top right. Floating center right: [OUR PRODUCT] at a slight angle, studio-lit. Below it: a
white rounded review card with five filled [ACCENT COLOR] stars and "[SHORT REVIEW QUOTE]" in
[BRAND COLOR] italic serif. Bottom center: brand logo in white.

Must be sourced: the quote and rating.
