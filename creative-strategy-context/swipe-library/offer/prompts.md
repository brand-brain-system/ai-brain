## From the Adcrate playbook

### #2 · Offer/Promotion split background
Suggested ratio: 9:16 (or 4:5 for feed)
Example image: `pb-02-offer-promotion.png`

Prompt: A promotional ad with a split background. Top 60% is [PRIMARY BRAND COLOR] and bottom 40%
is [CONTRAST COLOR like warm cream]. [OUR PRODUCT] sits centered where the colors meet, soft
studio lighting. Upper area: large [CONTRAST COLOR] sans-serif reading "[OFFER like YOUR FIRST
MONTH FREE]". Below: "[OFFER DETAILS]". Lower section: small [BRAND COLOR] text with "[VALUE
ADDS]". Brand logo bottom right.

Must be sourced: the offer, its terms and every value add (the brand's live site or brief, dated).

### #14 · Bundle showcase + benefit bar
Suggested ratio: 1:1 (the example is 4:5)
Example image: `pb-14-bundle-benefit-bar.png`

Prompt: A static ad on a [BACKGROUND like a soft gradient in brand colors] background. Top:
oversized bold white all-caps sans-serif headline "[HEADLINE like YOUR COMPLETE DAILY SYSTEM]".
Below it: a full-width [ACCENT COLOR] bar divided into [NUMBER] equal segments by thin vertical
lines, each holding a two-word benefit label in white: "[BENEFIT 1-5]". Center to bottom: [THE
BUNDLE like an open branded gift box, or the products grouped] from a slight overhead angle,
showing [NUMBER] [PRODUCTS], each a different [VARIANT]. Lower foreground: [LIFESTYLE PROP like a
hand holding one unit] entering from the bottom. Brand logo bottom left. Bright studio light,
saturated color, energetic. As an offer ad, add a line under the bar: "[BUNDLE OFFER like 3 for
499 kr.]".

Must be sourced: the benefit labels (brand materials); the bundle, its contents and price (live
offer, dated). The example shows no offer, so on its own it's a features ad; the offer line is what
makes it belong here.
