# Features & benefits: prompts

Rules for building the final prompt: `../production.md`. In this category, every benefit and
stat comes from the brand's own materials (product page, label, spec sheet). Customer counts and
review counts are social proof and follow the same sourcing rule.

## From the Adcrate playbook

### #4 · Point-out diagram
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-04-point-out.png`

Prompt: An educational diagram-style ad on a white background. Top: bold [BRAND COLOR] text
"[HEADER like What Makes PRODUCT Different]". Below: [OUR PRODUCT] centered, even studio lighting.
Four callout boxes with connecting lines: "[BENEFIT 1-4]", each with a small [BRAND COLOR] circle.
"[WEBSITE]" bottom center. Brand logo bottom right. A scientific diagram redesigned by a luxury
agency.

Must be sourced: the four benefits.

### #5 · Bullet points, split
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-05-bullet-points.png`

Prompt: A benefit-list ad, split composition on a [BACKGROUND] background. Left 40%: [OUR PRODUCT]
on [SURFACE], 85mm f/2.8. Right 60%: a vertical stack of five lines, each with a filled [BRAND
COLOR] circle: "[BENEFIT 1-5]". Clean sans-serif, generous spacing. Brand logo bottom right.

Must be sourced: the five benefits.

### #13 · Stat surround (product hero)
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-13-stat-surround-product.png`

Prompt: A white-to-[LIGHT GRADIENT COLOR] gradient background, fading top to bottom. Top: large
bold [TEXT COLOR] sans-serif headline "[HEADLINE]". Center: [OUR PRODUCT], soft studio light.
Floating near it: a small circular badge "[PRICE POINT like AS LOW AS X PER SERVING]" in [BADGE
COLOR]. On both sides: four stat callouts with hand-drawn-style curved [ARROW COLOR] arrows
pointing to the product. Left top: "[STAT 1]" oversized bold with "[LABEL 1]" below. Left bottom:
"[STAT 2]" / "[LABEL 2]". Right top: "[STAT 3]" / "[LABEL 3]". Right bottom: "[STAT 4]" /
"[LABEL 4]" with five gold stars if it's a rating. Bottom foreground: [FLAVOR OR INGREDIENT PROPS]
for appetite appeal. No logo. Clean, informational, appetizing.

Must be sourced: all four stats and the price point. The video's own run produced a stat
("7,600") the presenter thought the model made up; check every number in the output against the
approved copy.

### #18 · Stat surround (lifestyle flatlay)
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-18-stat-surround-flatlay.png`

Prompt: A white background with a lifestyle flatlay. Top: a bold [ACCENT COLOR] full-width banner
with white all-caps sans-serif "[HEADLINE]". Center: [PERSON DETAIL like a woman's hand] holding [OUR
PRODUCT] mid-frame. Around the edges: [FLATLAY PROPS] arranged organically, slightly out of focus.
Four stat callouts with curved [ACCENT COLOR] arrows pointing at the held product: "[STAT 1] /
[LABEL 1]" through "[STAT 4] / [LABEL 4]". Stats in bold black, labels in regular all-caps. Bright,
flat studio light. Energetic and appetizing.

Must be sourced: all four stats.

### #22 · Flavor story / "tastes like"
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-22-flavor-story.png`

Prompt: A flavor-visualization ad. The whole background is a photorealistic close-up food scene of
[INDULGENT FOOD the flavor evokes] on [SURFACE], 50mm f/2.8, shallow depth of field, warm bakery
light. Top third: large bold white sans-serif headline "[HEADLINE like A protein bar that tastes
like freshly baked raspberry donuts]" with one key word in bold italic. [OUR PRODUCT] bottom right,
angled 15° as if casually laid into the scene. Bottom: a semi-transparent white full-width bar with
three stat columns split by thin vertical lines: "[STAT 1]" | "[STAT 2]" | "[STAT 3]". Very bottom
edge: smaller bold sans-serif "[CLEAN-LABEL CLAIM]". The food is the hero, the product is the payoff.

Must be sourced: the three stats and the clean-label claim (nutrition label). The "tastes like"
line works best in customer language.

### #30 · Hero statement + icon benefit bar
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-30-hero-icon-bar.png`

Prompt: A bold statement ad. Top 15%: a white banner with a massive bold [BRAND COLOR] uppercase
sans-serif headline "[2-3 WORD STATEMENT]" with a full stop for punch. Middle 55%: a lifestyle
product photo, [SCENE], label clearly visible. Bottom 25%: [SOFT BRAND COLOR] background with three
evenly spaced icon-and-text columns: "[ICON 1 + LABEL]" | "[ICON 2 + LABEL]" | "[ICON 3 + LABEL]",
icons as simple line art in [BRAND COLOR] circles. Very bottom edge: a [DARK BRAND COLOR] ticker
bar repeating "[SOCIAL PROOF LINE]".

Must be sourced: the three labels and the social proof line. The playbook example ("Appetite
killer / Lose weight") is a health claim; only use claims the brand is allowed to make.

### #34 · Faux iPhone Notes screenshot
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-34-iphone-notes.png`

Prompt: A static ad disguised as an iPhone Notes screenshot. Top: a realistic iOS status bar (time
"[TIME]", signal, wifi, battery). Below: iOS Notes navigation, blue "< All iCloud" back arrow left,
share and three-dot icons right. Small gray timestamp "[DATE]". White content area: bold black
serif headline "[HEADLINE like In just one a day]". Below: three rows, each a [BRAND COLOR] filled
circle checkmark + [EMOJI] + bold black text in food-equivalency format: "[BENEFIT 1-3 like More
vitamin D than 800 mushrooms]". Right side, slightly overlapping the text: [OUR PRODUCT] at a
slight angle with [DETAILS like a few capsules spilling out], breaking the frame a little.

Must be sourced: every food-equivalency figure has to be calculable from the label, with the math
saved in the brand brain.

### #35 · Hero product + stat bar
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-35-hero-stat-bar.png`

Prompt: A product showcase on a [BACKGROUND COLOR like warm sand] background. Top: large bold [BRAND
COLOR] uppercase sans-serif headline "[CLAIM]". Below: a white rounded CTA button with [BRAND
COLOR] uppercase "[CTA]". Center: [OUR PRODUCT] in full packaging, angled slightly, hero-lit.
Around it: [SCATTERED ELEMENTS like broken pieces, ingredient crumbs] in an exploded radial pattern.
Bottom: a light rounded pill-shaped stat bar across the width with three metrics split by thin
vertical lines: "[STAT 1]" | "[STAT 2]" | "[STAT 3]" in bold [BRAND COLOR], numbers large, labels
small. Note: our rules put the CTA at the bottom, so move the button below the stat bar.

Must be sourced: the three stats. Superlative headlines ("the world's healthiest") need proof the
brand has; otherwise rewrite.

### #12 · Lifestyle action + product colorway array
Suggested ratio: 1:1
Example image: `pb-12-lifestyle-colorways.png`

Prompt: [LIFESTYLE PHOTO like a man mid-golf-swing wearing the product] fills the left two-thirds
of the frame, outdoors in [SETTING], bright natural daylight. Brand logo top center. Below it:
large bold sans-serif headline "[HEADLINE]" in [TEXT COLOR] with a subtle outline for legibility.
Bottom right foreground: three [PRODUCT VARIANTS like folded shirts] fanned and overlapping,
showing [COLOR 1], [COLOR 2] and [COLOR 3], crisp and studio-lit against the slightly softer
background. 50mm f/2.0. [MOOD like confident, athletic, aspirational but accessible].

Must be sourced: a headline in quotation marks is a real review or press quote with its source;
otherwise drop the quotation marks. The colorways exist in the shop.
