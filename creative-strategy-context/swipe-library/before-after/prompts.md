## From the Adcrate playbook

### #8 · UGC-native mirror selfie before/after
Suggested ratio: 9:16
Example image: `pb-08-ugc-mirror-selfie.png`
Reference use: product color only. This should look like a real person's post.

Prompt: A TikTok before-and-after. LEFT: a grainy iPhone mirror selfie, [PERSON] in a dimly lit
bathroom, [BEFORE STATE], harsh lighting. White handwritten text: "[BEFORE DATE]". RIGHT: the same
person in the same bathroom, bright natural light, [AFTER STATE], [OUR PRODUCT] visible on the
counter. White text: "[AFTER DATE]". Top center: "[TIMEFRAME] on [BRAND]" with an emoji. Should look
stitched together in CapCut.

Must be sourced: the before/after states and timeframe (a result the brand claims). Concept only
unless the photos are a real customer's.

### #36 · Whiteboard before/after + product hold
Suggested ratio: 4:5
Example image: `pb-36-whiteboard.png`
Reference use: product packaging only. This should look like a real person's photo.

Prompt: A lifestyle photo in [SETTING like a bright modern kitchen]. In the background: a small
tabletop whiteboard propped up on [SURFACE]. On it, two simple hand-drawn black marker line
drawings side by side: left labeled "[BEFORE LABEL]" showing [BEFORE STATE], an arrow pointing
right to a second drawing labeled "[AFTER LABEL]" showing [AFTER STATE]. Below the drawings,
handwritten in black marker: "[HANDWRITTEN LINE]". Foreground lower right: [PERSON'S HAND] holding
[OUR PRODUCT] up next to the whiteboard, label clearly visible. Shot on iPhone, natural kitchen
light, casual and educational.

Must be sourced: the before/after states and the handwritten line (brand claims or real customer
language). Drawings sidestep the need for real photos, but the claim still has to be real.
