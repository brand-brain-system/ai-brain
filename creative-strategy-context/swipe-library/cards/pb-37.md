## Body

**Layout forensics.** This is the promo remix of a plainer hero-statement template: a white band across the very top carries a short, punchy two-word claim in bold dark-green uppercase, ended with a period for punch. Below that the background drops to dark, moody charcoal. Upper-left, a neon-green comic-style starburst badge sits rotated off-axis with a bold black discount callout inside it — the one saturated, attention-grabbing color note on an otherwise dark frame. Center-frame, a hand grips the product jar mid-lift, pulling it up off its retail box below, lit dramatically from the side rather than flat studio light. A semi-transparent dark strip near the bottom carries three evenly spaced icon-and-label columns, simple line-art icons in neon circles over bold white uppercase benefit words. The very bottom edge is a full-width neon banner naming the promo occasion. Eye path: the white-banded headline first (highest contrast against the dark field below it), then the product being lifted (the largest, most dynamic gesture in the frame), then the starburst badge pulling the eye back up-left toward the number that makes it worth clicking.

**The story this ad tells.** It wants the viewer to believe this is a limited, worth-acting-on moment on a product that already works — the plain statement headline supplies the core promise, the starburst supplies urgency-adjacent value (a real percentage off), the lifted-product gesture supplies energy, and the three benefit icons remind the viewer why they wanted this in the first place before the discount ever entered the picture. This layout carries a real, verified discount on top of an already-proven benefit set best — it's built for promo periods, not evergreen, and it needs the benefit claims doing real work underneath the discount rather than the discount being the only reason to look.

**Copy skeleton.** headline — hook: land a short, punchy category claim; statement; 2 words. badge — offer: state the real discount range; number + command; 5 words. list — prove: remind them of the core benefits before the discount closes the sale; benefit ×3; 6 words across 3 items. offer — tell them what to do: name the promo occasion; statement; 3 words.

**Adaptation notes.** Needs a real, currently-live discount range and three benefits the brand can already stand behind outside of promo periods — this template assumes the underlying product claim is proven and just adds urgency on top, so it shouldn't be the first time those three benefits are being asserted. Legal swap that keeps the layout intact: swap the discount percentage and promo name for whatever is actually live (seasonal sale name, different percentage band). Swap that breaks it: inflating the discount number beyond what's actually offered, or borrowing a promo name (Black Friday, a named sale event) that isn't genuinely running — the banner reads as a real, checkable claim to the viewer.
