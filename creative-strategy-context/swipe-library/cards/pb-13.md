## Body

**Layout forensics.**

A soft white-to-color gradient fades top to bottom behind the entire frame. Across the top, a bold two-line headline in the brand's accent color spans nearly the full width, setting up a taste-versus-health tension. Dead center, a single product sits front-facing on a clean studio-lit surface, large enough to read its own front-of-pack design. A small white circular badge overlaps the top corner of the product, carrying a bold price claim. Flanking the product on both sides, four oversized stat callouts sit in the corners — two on the left, two on the right, one paired with a small star rating — each linked to the product by a thin curved arrow so the numbers and the product stay visually connected despite the spacing. In the foreground, a loose, scatterable version of the product sits below it, adding texture without adding more text. First thing the eye lands on: the product itself, the single largest, most central, most in-focus object on the page. Second: the four stat numbers ringing it, since their scale rivals the headline and the arrows explicitly route the eye back to the product. Third: the headline at the top, which frames everything below it.

**The story this ad tells.**

This ad tells the viewer "the numbers back up the taste claim," using the product as a literal center of gravity that four hard facts physically point at. The headline sets up a tension that the stat ring resolves with specifics instead of adjectives. Image and words split the work: the product photo and scattered props sell appetite, while the four numbers do all of the actual reason-to-believe work. This layout carries fact-dense, scannable product claims best for food and supplement categories with real numbers to put on the page — it's built to be understood in under two seconds even by someone not reading closely.

**Copy skeleton.**

- headline: hook (sets up the tension the stats resolve), statement, 7 words
- badge: hook (a price-per-serving claim, makes the offer concrete), stat, 6 words
- stat (left-upper): prove (a positive nutrition number), stat, 2 words
- stat (left-lower): prove (a positive nutrition number), stat, 2 words
- stat (right-upper): prove (a count framed as a positive), stat, 2 words
- stat (right-lower): prove (review volume plus a star rating), stat, 3 words

**Adaptation notes.**

Needs four real, verifiable numbers — nutrition facts and/or review counts — this template cannot run on invented or rounded-for-effect stats, and dropping to three stats leaves an obviously empty corner since the layout is built symmetrically around four. Needs a single hero packshot with the pack design readable at this scale, plus a loose, scatterable version of the product or a category-appropriate prop for the foreground. Swaps that keep the layout intact: which four stats are chosen, the price badge, the scattered foreground prop. Swaps that break it: fewer than four stats, or any stat that isn't independently verifiable. Flag for human review: every number on this card is a claim and needs source-checking before it runs, especially in supplement or better-for-you food categories where substantiation rules apply.

**Fit note.** food/supplement/consumable with real numbers | TOF | 4 verified stats | informational-appetizing tone.
