**Layout forensics.** A white surface is fully covered by a flatlay: a bold black banner bar spans the top ~18% of the frame, white all-caps sans headline set across two centered lines. Center-frame, a stand-up black pouch sits behind a two-tone shaker bottle gripped by a hand and forearm entering from the bottom edge. Four stat callouts are scattered clockwise around the product — upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, lower-right — each a bold black numeral over a smaller all-caps label, connected to the product by a thin black curved arrow. The remaining negative space at every edge is filled with soft-focus flatlay props: vanilla pods, oats, banana slices, almonds, a filled cup, and extra product pouches in other flavors, deliberately blurred so they read as texture, not competing focal points. Eye order: the black headline banner first (hardest contrast on the page), then the held product at dead center, then the stat callouts pulling the eye around the product in a loop.

**The story this ad tells.** It wants the viewer to believe this is a fast, credentialed, appetizing meal-replacement — the headline makes the speed/completeness promise, the four stats back it with hard numbers, and the flatlay of real food props makes a powdered product feel like actual breakfast. Image and words work as a system: the arrows physically route proof back to the product being held mid-use, so nothing reads as decoration. This layout carries dense, verifiable spec claims best — protein, calories, vitamin count, scale — anywhere the brand has real numbers to back a "why trust this over a bowl of cereal" pitch.

**Copy skeleton.**
- headline — hook — bold claim, command-adjacent statement — 6 words
- stat (×4) — prove — numeral + label, arrow-anchored — 11 words across 4 items

**Adaptation notes.** Needs four real, defensible numbers (protein grams, calorie count, ingredient/vitamin count, a scale or trust number like units sold) — inventing precision here is a claims risk. Needs a genuine hand-hold product shot and real flatlay props matched to the category (swap almonds/oats/banana for whatever the brand's flavor profile actually is — that swap keeps the layout intact). Swap-breaking: dropping to fewer than four stats collapses the "surrounded by proof" composition the arrows are built around, and losing the hand from the frame turns a lived-in lifestyle shot back into a plain packshot. A real hand is rendered here — flag for content-filter review on hand/finger anatomy before shipping.
