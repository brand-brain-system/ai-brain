## How many ads

1. **Quick rule:** about one new ad per month per 1,000 AUD of monthly spend (roughly 4,000-4,500
   kr.; check the rate). A starting point when there's no history, nothing more
2. **Better, from the brand's own history:** new-customer revenue target ÷ average return per ad =
   ads needed. Example with invented numbers: 500 ads spent 500,000 kr. in a year, so 1,000 kr. per ad;
   at ROAS 3 that's 3,000 kr. back per ad; a target of 300,000 kr. new-customer revenue a month
   needs about 100 new ads a month
3. **Two corrections the quick maths misses:** ads take time to spend (if the average ad spends
   over 3 months, this month's target rests partly on last quarter's launches), and winners and
   losers are very different (a month of losers earns almost nothing). With enough history, model
   winners and losers separately: hit rate × average winner return + the rest × average loser return
4. **Per concept:** at a 5 % hit rate, a new concept needs 10-20 ads before a winner is likely

Volume only counts above the quality bar. An account launching thousands of weak ads a month is
busy, not strategic: the bar is the copy checks and persona panel in the skills, and "what's
worked" in `brand-lens.md`. Above the bar, nobody reliably picks the winner, so launch them all.

## How to split the effort

| Share | Content type | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 50-60 % | Winners remade | New hooks on proven bodies, a proven message in a new format, a proven script with a new creator |
| 20-30 % | Iterations | Fixing near-misses (hook, bridge), a working concept with a new persona or offer |
| 20-30 % | New concepts | New angles and personas, from the gaps in `strategy/strategic-roadmap.md` and `idea-bank/index.md` |

Never 0 % new: every winner fatigues, and the next ones come from here. Early on, with no winners
yet, most of the effort is new concepts by necessity.

Two other mixes to check:
- **Awareness:** most of the production (70-90 %) aimed at people who don't know the brand yet;
  the rest at people who do. Retargeting ads each answer one objection (price, trust, quality, not
  ready yet), in proportion to how often each comes up in `personas/voice-of-customer/voice-of-customer.md`
- **Pain vs. desire:** ads that start from a problem vs. ads that only make the product look
  desirable (the campaign-shoot style). He puts the best performance brands at about 60-70 % pain-led.
  `persona-gap` Mode B counts it

## Does production pay?

Per source (static, UGC, edit, AI): average contribution margin per ad (average return per ad minus
product cost and the ad spend itself) against cost per asset. If a video costs more to make than the
margin the average video earns, make more of what does pay, often statics. Track it monthly.

His rough budget for production, as a share of Meta spend (AUD per month):
- under 30,000: the founder makes it; time, not money
- 30,000-100,000: about 25 %
- above 100,000: falling towards about 10 %

A brand that treats creative as a cost and media spend as the revenue driver has it backwards: the
ads find the customers, Meta only distributes them. Useful framing for a client who won't fund
production. Say it with their own numbers, not his.
